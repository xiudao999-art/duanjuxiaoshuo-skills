# Multi-storyline template system

Attempt only templates supported by the full-series evidence. A normal drama should yield 8–10 active families, not force every family.

| ID | Family | Central question pattern | Preferred evidence | Typical variants |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| F01 | Continuous conflict window | How does one local crisis escalate and resolve? | Adjacent causal scenes | discovery, escalation, reversal, aftermath |
| F02 | Protagonist agency line | How does the lead change from passive to active? | decisions, costs, capability proofs | awakening, counterattack, sacrifice |
| F03 | Important supporting-character line | What does this character want and what price do they pay? | viewpoint scenes, choices, consequences | ally, rival, mother, child, mentor |
| F04 | Love/relationship line | What separates or reunites the pair? | attraction, misunderstanding, protection, confession | first trust, rupture, reconciliation |
| F05 | Family/filial line | What is hidden inside care, duty, or resentment? | caregiving, sacrifice, accusation, recognition | mother-child, siblings, chosen family |
| F06 | Betrayal/revenge line | Who betrayed whom and how is the debt repaid? | setup, proof, loss, counterattack | emotional, financial, status, survival |
| F07 | Identity/reveal line | Who is the person really and who benefits from concealment? | clues, misrecognition, verification, reveal | hidden identity, switched role, inheritance |
| F08 | Mystery/foreshadowing line | What unexplained clue connects distant events? | repeated clue, object, witness, contradiction | culprit, disappearance, secret history |
| F09 | Villain causality line | How does the antagonist build and lose control? | motive, manipulation, collateral cost, exposure | conspiracy, coercion, hypocrisy |
| F10 | Action/rescue/face-slap line | Can the lead survive, rescue, or reverse humiliation? | threat, physical stakes, intervention, consequence | fight, rescue, courtroom, public reversal |
| F11 | Contrast/mirror line | Why do two people facing the same choice become opposites? | matched situations and different decisions | mother vs mother, rich vs poor, past vs present |
| F12 | Object/location line | How does one object or place carry the truth? | recurring prop/location and changing meaning | letter, medicine, photo, home, hospital |
| F13 | Emotional anthology | What do all grief/love/comedy beats reveal together? | coherent thematic moments with escalation | all love, all tears, all comedy |
| F14 | Alternate viewpoint | How does the same conflict look from another character? | scenes where viewpoint changes causal meaning | mother, child, antagonist, witness |

For each active family, derive multiple narrative windows only when the central question and payoff are distinct. A family label alone is not differentiation.

After choosing a family, select and record a `narrative_profile` from
[narrative-profiles.md](narrative-profiles.md). The family describes what the
story is about; the profile describes how that particular window tells it.
