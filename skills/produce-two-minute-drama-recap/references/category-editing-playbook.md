# Category-specific narrative and editing playbook

Use this reference after a candidate has been bound to one `F01`–`F10`
family. These rules were validated against five local short-drama samples in
`D:/video-grap/douyin-mcp-node/output/videos/短剧样例` on 2026-08-07.

Do not apply every pattern to every recap. Select one primary pattern that
matches the candidate's governing viewer question, then add at most one
compatible secondary pattern. A rule proven for one family is not a global
editing default.

## F01 Causal plot window

### `two_line_convergence`

Use when one character obtains proof while another enters danger.

```text
unexpected bond
→ one party leaves
→ A obtains evidence
↔ B loses options
→ phone or prop joins the lines
→ immediate action
```

- Every location change needs a departure, call, document, trace, or pursuit
  action as its bridge.
- End at arrival, breach, or the first decisive action.

### `limited_explanatory_flashback`

- A flashback explains only the direct cause of the current abnormality.
- Keep it around 10–25% of the recap.
- Enter and exit with an explicit time bridge.
- Reuse the flashback's defining action at the payoff when possible.

### `dual_time_backfill`

```text
current conflict result
→ distant cause
→ immediate recent cause
→ return to current action
```

- Both backfills must answer the same current conflict.
- Use no more than three time layers in the first 30 seconds.
- Speak the time bridge before or on the first frame of the new period.

### `long_window_causal_expansion`

Expand in this order only:

```text
old personal goal
→ cause of the abnormality
→ evidence of new responsibility
→ antagonist's concrete gain
→ crisis transmission
→ ability兑现/payoff
```

Reject biography, repeated emotion, or side mysteries that do not alter a
later action.

### F01 speech and picture rule

Continuous narration may cross frequent visual cuts only when every incoming
shot visibly proves the current noun, action, consequence, or time bridge.
Do not copy this density into a silent performance-led character scene.

## F02 Character strategy or transformation

### `freedom_to_responsibility`

```text
small personal goal
→ forced relationship interruption
→ hesitation
→ vulnerable person's need or injury
→ voluntary fact check
→ protection action
```

- Preserve the old goal so the viewer can see change.
- Do not narrate that the character matured; prove it with investigation,
  care, sacrifice, or rescue.

## F03 Relationship state transition

### `forced_bond_to_voluntary_care`

Use for forced marriage, child-seeking-parent, or contract relationships.
Move from involuntary contact through shared evidence to a voluntary
protective action.

### `old_debt_reactivated`

Use one past rescue or favor as the unfinished relationship, then let a new
need force the characters to renegotiate that bond.

### `child_as_relationship_agent`

Each child appearance must perform one different function: identify the
relationship, expose an old cost, show a family wish, correct the adult
relationship direction, transmit danger, or trigger responsibility. Delete
repeated cute reactions that change nothing.

### `wish_dialogue_island`

- At an actual relationship-state change, pause narration for 5–10 seconds.
- Preserve the complete question, answer, and receiving reaction.
- Resume narration only after the utterance and reaction finish.

### `promise_action_verification`

```text
explicit relationship wish
→ complete promise
→ real responsibility tests it
→ voluntary investigation or care
→ protection action fulfills it
```

The second half must visibly act on the same person named in the promise.

## F04 Antagonist mechanism

### `interest_ledger`

- State the beneficiary, exchange condition, and asset being taken.
- Prefer a contract, shares, project, controlled access, or coercive action to
  repeated insults.
- Use for marriage deals, inheritance, company seizure, and family extraction.

### `escape_route_removal`

```text
deception into location
→ exit blocked
→ body controlled
→ vulnerable person threatened
→ final perpetrator arrives
```

- Each level must remove one additional escape option.
- Keep one strongest action or complete line per level.
- Delete synonymous abuse and repeated pulling at the same threat level.

## F05 Ability or credibility proof

### `action_before_title`

Show a successful fight, rescue, treatment, or solution before a subordinate
confirms the title. Do not open with an identity encyclopedia.

### `command_fulfillment_chain`

After the title, issue a testable command. Later show the investigation,
location result, evidence delivery, or personnel arrival. A kneeling shot
alone does not prove operational power.

## F06 Rule, resource, or economic game

### `marriage_asset_leverage`

```text
marriage becomes transaction entry
→ powerful family supplies status or access
→ original family transfers shares or project
→ target loses personal and asset control
```

The viewer must understand what moves from whom to whom.

### `dual_value_resource`

Use when one resource has an economic value to the antagonist and a life,
relationship, or moral value to the protagonist. Show both values. In the
validated sample, the medicine project was both a saleable asset and the
child's only treatment hope.

## F07 Action, crisis, rescue, or public counterattack

### `double_countdown_rescue`

The victim line continuously loses options while the rescuer line gains proof,
location, and permission to act. Converge in the final 15–25 seconds.

### `rescue_action_callback`

When the protagonist saved the same person before, echo one action, framing,
or line during the new rescue. The callback must carry changed stakes.

### `witness_transmission`

Keep a weak witness inside the danger line and use a call, voice note, or
location share to join the two spaces.

### `complete_distress_dialogue_island`

- Place the call immediately after relationship or responsibility confirmation.
- Preserve 7–12 seconds when needed for a complete distress sentence and the
  receiver's reaction.
- Include at least one of location, aggressor, victim, or concrete danger.
- Narration resumes only after the complete crying sentence.

### `escape_ladder_compression`

- Victim line: restricted exit → physical control → vulnerable-person threat
  → final perpetrator arrival.
- Rescuer line: responsibility confirmation → distress signal → location →
  mobilization → arrival.
- Keep one strongest visual per level. Every intercut must change risk or
  distance.

## F08 Identity, mystery, clue, or reveal

### `progressive_identity_levels`

Move from ordinary cover through past action and relationship identity to
hidden power. Each level needs an action, prop, title, or other person's
reaction.

### `commitment_before_power_reveal`

Let the protagonist accept responsibility before revealing power. The first
power action must serve that responsibility.

### `audience_knows_first`

Reveal the identity to the viewer while other characters remain unaware.
Change the question from “who is this” to “when will it be exposed and used.”
Delay fulfillment through at least one responsibility test or escalation.

### `one_window_one_primary_mystery`

Do not make lost-heir identity, organization leadership, parentage, and a
hidden-enemy investigation four equal questions in a two-minute recap. Pick
one. Other identity facts remain only when they directly change the current
choice. End with confirmation or irreversible new proof.

## F09 Motif, prop, phrase, or behavior recurrence

### `prop_relay`

Assign different stages to different props: emotional need, identity proof,
then crisis transmission. Repeated close-ups without a new function are not a
motif progression.

### `address_term_progression`

```text
mistaken “dad”
→ wish for a father
→ protagonist accepts the address and promises
→ distress call uses the address
→ action fulfills its meaning
```

Every recurrence must change the relationship meaning.

## F10 Alternate perspective, contrast, irony, or comedy

### `ordinary_cover_real_power`

Stabilize the ordinary job through real work actions, then contrast it with
subordinates, resource dispatch, or precise operational control. Narration
alone cannot create the contrast.

### `dangerous_action_romantic_semantics`

Use a threatening prop or approach with dialogue that resolves into proposal,
jealousy, or relationship confirmation within 3–8 seconds. This is only for
light relationship comedy; never apply it to actual abuse or serious harm.

### `comedy_before_crisis_only`

Self-flattery, flirt misunderstanding, and ordinary-job jokes belong before
illness, coercion, or child danger becomes active. Do not restore comedy until
the current crisis is fully resolved.

## Category-specific audio QC

For F03 and F07, never delete a range only because ASR reports silence when
any of these are true:

- burned-in source subtitles show dialogue;
- mouth movement continues without recognized words;
- music, crying, and low dialogue overlap;
- a recognized question has no recognized answer.

Review picture, waveform, source subtitles, and dialogue closure together.
Keep the complete first and last spoken word plus 0.30–0.50 seconds of reaction
space when no new utterance begins.
