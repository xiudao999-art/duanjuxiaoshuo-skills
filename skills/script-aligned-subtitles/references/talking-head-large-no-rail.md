# Talking-head large captions without a rail

Use `talking-head-large-no-rail-1080x1920` for presenter-led, interview,
lecture, podcast, and other talking-head footage. At 1080x1920 lock normal text
to 88 px, highlights to 96 px, baseline to 1450, and two-line gap to 160 px.
Do not add a blur, dark strip, color card, or source-subtitle-removal rail.
